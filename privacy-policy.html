Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This policy explains what data zalgo-text.org collects, what it does not collect, and how third parties are involved.

The text you type is never uploaded

Every generator on this site runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Whatever you type is processed on your own device and never transmitted to us.

This is a structural property, not a policy promise — there is no server-side endpoint that receives your text, so there is nothing to log, store or leak. It also means the tools keep working if your connection drops after the page has loaded.

We do not ask for personal information

There are no accounts on this site. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to use any tool. If you email us voluntarily, we use your address only to reply.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, standard technical information may be collected for operation and analytics: browser type, operating system, approximate country-level location, pages visited, referring site and time of visit. This is used in aggregate to understand which tools are useful and is not used to identify you.

Cookies

Cookies may be used to remember preferences and to measure site performance. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the tools on this site will continue to work normally without them.

Third-party advertising

We may display advertising through third-party networks such as Google AdSense. These networks use their own cookies — including the DART cookie — to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings or via the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page. We do not control third-party privacy practices and recommend reviewing their policies directly.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Because we do not operate accounts or store the text you enter, we typically hold no personal data about visitors. For questions about advertising data, contact the advertising network directly.

External links

This site links to external websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices and encourage you to read the policy of any site you visit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top will change accordingly. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@zalgo-text.org or the contact page.
