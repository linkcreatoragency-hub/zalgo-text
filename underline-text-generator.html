How it works

Unlike the fancy-font styles, this effect does not replace your letters. It keeps them exactly as they are and inserts a zero-width combining mark after each one. The mark has no width, so it draws on top of the character that came before it.

That has a useful consequence: the underlying text is unchanged. Search still matches it, screen readers still read the words, and stripping the marks restores the original perfectly — which is exactly what the Zalgo to normal text tool does.

Single, double or overline?

  • Underline (u̲n̲d̲e̲r̲l̲i̲n̲e̲) — the standard single rule. Best support, cleanest rendering.
  • Double underline (d̳o̳u̳b̳l̳e̳) — two rules. Traditionally used in accounting for final totals and in proofreading to mark small capitals.
  • Overline (o̅v̅e̅r̅l̅i̅n̅e̅) — a line above instead of below. Used in mathematics for repeating decimals and logical negation.

A word of caution about underlines

Online, an underline means one thing to most readers: this is a link. Decades of web convention have made that association automatic, and underlining text that is not clickable produces a small moment of confusion every time someone reads it.

That is not a reason never to use it, but it is a reason to prefer bold for ordinary emphasis and keep underlines for cases where the meaning is clear from context — a heading in a bio, a total in a list, a deliberately old-fashioned typewriter look.

Things to know before you commit

The trade-offs worth knowing

  • Search engines and site search may not match it. To a computer, 𝓱𝓮𝓵𝓵𝓸 is not the word hello. Keep your searchable text plain.
  • Screen readers handle it badly. Many announce the characters one by one or skip them, so avoid styling text that carries important information.
  • Some fonts lack the glyphs. Where a device has no glyph you get a box (□). Coverage is very good on modern phones and desktops, weaker on older devices and some smart TVs.
  • A few platforms filter it from names. Facebook and some games restrict which characters may appear in a real-name field.

None of that matters for a bio, a caption or a username. All of it matters for body text, headings on your own website, or anything you want found in search.

Frequently asked questions

How do I underline text on Instagram?

Instagram has no underline button. Type your text above, copy the underlined version and paste it into your bio or caption.

Does underlined text stay searchable?

Yes. Only invisible combining marks are added; the letters themselves are unchanged.

What is a double underline used for?

In accounting it marks a final total, and in proofreading it marks text to be set in small capitals.

Why does the line have small gaps?

Each mark attaches to a single character, so some fonts leave hairline breaks between them.

Can I combine underline with bold?

Not in one pass. Convert to bold first, then paste the bold result back in to underline it.

How do I remove the underline?

Paste the text into the Zalgo to normal text tool, which strips all combining marks.
